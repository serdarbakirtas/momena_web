Cookie Policy

Last Updated: March 18, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience. We also use similar technologies such as localStorage to store your cookie consent choice.

2. How We Use Cookies

This website uses a minimal set of cookies and local storage:

  • Consent preference: We store your choice (Accept or Reject) in your browser's localStorage so we don't show the cookie banner again. This is essential for the consent mechanism to work.
  • Google Fonts: We load fonts from Google Fonts. Google may set cookies when fonts are requested. You can review Google's privacy policy for details.

We do not use:

  • Advertising or tracking cookies
  • Analytics cookies
  • Third-party marketing cookies

3. Your Choices

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies using our cookie banner. If you reject, we will only use storage necessary to remember your rejection.

You can change your preference at any time by clicking "Cookie preferences" in the footer, which will show the consent banner again.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at serdarbakirtas@pm.me.
