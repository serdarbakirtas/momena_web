What the subscription includes

iCloud Backup lets you store an encrypted copy of your Momena data in your own iCloud account so you can restore it when you change devices. Core features—memories, calendar, and health tracking stored on-device—do not require a subscription.

Prices are charged in your local currency where applicable; Apple may show a slightly different price after tax or rounding. Managing or canceling your subscription is done in your Apple ID settings.
