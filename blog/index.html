Welcome to the Momena blog

April 4, 2026

We are glad you are here. This space is where we share how Momena evolves, how we think about privacy, and practical tips for capturing memories without the clutter. The app is built around one idea: your family information should stay under your control, with encryption on-device and optional iCloud Backup when you want peace of mind across devices.

If there is a topic you would like us to cover, reach out on the Support page.

Why local-first matters for families

April 4, 2026

Many apps send your photos and notes to company servers by default. Momena starts from your iPhone or iPad: memories and health notes live encrypted on your device. You can use the app fully offline. If you subscribe to iCloud Backup, a copy is stored in your own iCloud account—not on our servers—so you can restore when you upgrade your phone.
