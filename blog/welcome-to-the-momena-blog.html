Welcome to the Momena blog

April 4, 2026 · …

We are glad you are here. This space is where we share how Momena evolves, how we think about privacy, and practical tips for capturing memories without the clutter.

What we write about

You can expect honest product updates when we ship meaningful changes, short notes on how we handle data, and ideas that fit real family routines—not growth hacks or noisy marketing.

The app is built around one idea: your family information should stay under your control. Memories, calendar moments, and health notes are encrypted on your iPhone or iPad first. When you want peace of mind across devices, optional iCloud Backup stores a copy in your iCloud account, not on our servers.
