Where Does Your Child's Data Actually Live?

April 12, 2026 · 4 min read

You downloaded a popular baby tracking app. You're logging your child's sleep schedule, feeding times, height, and weight. The app works great.

But where is that data?

Most apps upload by default

The vast majority of large app companies upload your data to their own cloud servers. They usually mention this deep in the privacy policy — where no one reads it.

What does this mean?

  • Your child's growth data is sitting on a server somewhere
  • Your sleep log records are being analyzed
  • Some apps share this data with third parties — ad networks, research companies

Your child's health data. For advertising purposes.

Is "anonymous" enough?

Some companies say "your data is anonymized." But research shows that "anonymous" datasets can be re-identified far more easily than you'd think — especially when birth date, location, and growth data are combined.

What makes Momena different?

In Momena, your data stays on your device by default. Nothing is automatically sent to the cloud.

  • Protected with AES-256 encryption
  • No tracking, no analytics
  • No ad networks
  • We don't see, share, or sell your data

If you enable iCloud Backup (with Momena Plus), your backups go to your own iCloud account — encrypted — not to our servers.

Why does this matter?

Because your child's health and development data is sensitive information. You have the right to know where it lives and who can access it.

Momena takes a clear stance on this: Your data, on your device.
